- Restore: 556 GiB unpacked, every chunk's MD5 matched its ETag, no retries. Throughput ran from 19 MB/s in the first minute to 50–72 MB/s.
- Catch-up: shard 2's snapshot was about 26 h old when the node started (94,660 s behind). The node closed that gap at about 3.8 s of chain time per second. Shard 2 is the slowest shard to catch up because its restore starts last.
- After sync: 557 GiB used of 984 GiB on the volume. snapchain 0.14.3.

Lesson for the next runs: snapshot age at node start decides the catch-up time. Shard 1's snapshot is published around 16:00 UTC and shard 2's around 10:00 UTC, so starting `./threecast node` in the evening (UTC) leaves the oldest shard least stale, and the node is synced by the morning.
